I'm an applied statistician with a Ph.D. from UCLA. My dissertation developed Bayesian methods for social and economic networks, the kind of models that describe how friendships and trade relationships form. As a research data scientist in psychometrics, I built hierarchical Bayesian models that track students' latent skills over time. The projects column contains project summaries and released software.

At Farmers Insurance I trained gradient boosted risk models that now inform optimal pricing and segment auto accident risk. My strength is building experimentation and causal inference frameworks to learn what works, Bayesian models that quantify the uncertainty, and machine learning systems that put them to use.
